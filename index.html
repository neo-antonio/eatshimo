Food Library

Log your meals and food calories, and optionally macros including protein, carbohydrates, fat, and even fiber.

Explore our Presets for common foods and Filipino dishes to quickly build your food library.

Daily Logger

Log your daily meals, water intake, steps, sleep, and weight.

We know not all meals are built the same — when you add less meat or use different vegetable sizes, you can edit the grams, calories, and macros individually for each logged meal without affecting your food library.

Profile

Enter your gender, height, and current weight to get calorie target suggestions for weight loss, gain, or maintenance. You can also set daily goals for your macros.

All data is stored locally on your device. Export or import your profile, food library, and meal logs as a CSV file to transfer or back up your data.
